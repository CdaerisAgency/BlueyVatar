### Fixed
- When the saved link to your account's home server cannot be renewed, the profile page now says so plainly and shows a Sign in again link, instead of a message that reads like you were signed out of the site.
- The signed-in header and footer refresh when you press Back to a page your browser kept in memory, so they no longer show a stale signed-in state after you sign out.
- Two servers can no longer renew the same account's connection at the same time, which could end the saved session and force a new sign-in.
