# Stripe billing

Stripe is the payment processor (issue #67). The web app sells Plus ($9 per month) and Business ($49 per month) through Checkout. Plus is one combined plan: it replaces the old Plus ($9.99) and Pro ($9) plans, and Pro is no longer sold and manages them through the Customer Portal. Until the first four variables below are set (the secret key, the webhook secret, and the two price ids), `/account/billing` says billing is unavailable, no Stripe call is made, and `/api/stripe/webhook` answers 503 naming the missing variables. Use a Stripe test-mode account first, then repeat the steps in live mode.

### Environment variables (web app)

| Variable | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` | Secret or restricted API key (`sk_...` or `rk_...`). A restricted key needs write on Checkout Sessions, Customer portal sessions, Customers, and Subscriptions (plan switching and cancel use the Subscriptions API; confirm the exact permission names in test mode). |
| `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET` | Signing secret (`whsec_...`) of the webhook endpoint created in step 5. |
| `STRIPE_PRICE_PLUS` | Price id (`price_...`) of the Plus monthly price ($9). Checkout and plan switching use the first id. To keep existing subscribers on retired prices working, append their ids after a comma (the old $9.99 Plus price and the old $9 Pro price). The webhook maps every listed id to Plus and never errors on them. |
| `STRIPE_PRICE_BUSINESS` | Price id (`price_...`) of the Business monthly price ($49). |
| `STRIPE_ALLOW_PROMO_CODES` | Optional. Set to `true` to show the promotion code box in Checkout. Any other value or unset keeps it hidden. |

Keep them in Infisical and push them with `fly secrets set --app <web app>`. They are never committed. `PUBLIC_URL` must be `https://blueyvatar.com` in production, since Checkout and portal return URLs are built from it.

### Dashboard steps

1. **Products and prices.** Create two products, "blueyvatar Plus" and "blueyvatar Business". Give each one recurring monthly price in USD: $9.00 for Plus and $49.00 for Business, with tax behavior set to Exclusive (tax is added on top). Copy each price id into `STRIPE_PRICE_PLUS` and `STRIPE_PRICE_BUSINESS`. Do not delete or archive the old Plus and Pro prices while people still subscribe to them: list their ids after the new Plus id in `STRIPE_PRICE_PLUS` (comma separated) until those subscribers have moved.
2. **Stripe Tax.** Settings, Tax: set the business head office address, add the registrations where tax must be collected, and set the default product tax category to "Software as a service (SaaS)" or the category counsel confirms. Checkout sends `automatic_tax` and collects the billing address and optional tax ID, so tax is computed per customer.
3. **Invoices.** Settings, Billing, Invoices: set the business name, address, support email, and invoice footer, and turn on customer emails for receipts and invoices. Stripe Billing creates an invoice for every subscription payment, with tax shown.
4. **Customer Portal.** Settings, Billing, Customer portal: enable payment method updates, invoice history, and subscription cancellation (at period end). Plan switching and cancel/resume are also built into `/account/billing`, so the portal is mainly for the card and invoices. Turn "Customers can switch plans" off, so downgrades go through the app and its end-of-period schedule. If it stays on, set its downgrade proration to apply at the end of the billing period (the portal can create a schedule itself, which the Stripe subscription schedules page confirms); how it behaves with the app's schedule is Unknown, and the app leaves a schedule it did not make alone. Set the business information and the return link to `https://blueyvatar.com/account/billing`. Save the default configuration.
5. **Webhook endpoint.** Developers, Webhooks, Add endpoint with the URL `https://blueyvatar.com/api/stripe/webhook`. Subscribe it to exactly these events and no others (the last two are new with downgrade at period end): `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `subscription_schedule.updated`, `subscription_schedule.completed`. Copy the signing secret into `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. For local testing, run `stripe listen --forward-to localhost:3000/api/stripe/webhook` and use the secret it prints.
6. **Payment retries.** Settings, Billing, Subscriptions and emails: use Smart Retries and email customers about failed payments. Set "If all retries for a payment fail" to cancel the subscription, and keep the retry window within the 7 day grace period below.
7. **Migrations.** Migration 0015 (billing tables and `licenses.grace_ends_at`) and migration 0017 (allows `plus` in `subscriptions.tier`) run with the delivery release command, along with 0016 (`email_links.email_domain`). Combining Plus and Pro itself adds no migration; migration 0036 stores the Plus license tier as `plus` (see Migrations). Deploy delivery first, then web.
8. **Smoke test in test mode.** Subscribe with card 4242 4242 4242 4242, confirm the license shows Tier: plus on `/account/keys`, `/account/billing` shows Plan: Plus, and the gallery section on `/account` unlocks. Cancel in the portal and confirm it returns to Free. Use card 4000 0000 0000 0341 to see a failed renewal move the license to past due.

### Plan changes in the app

`/account/billing` lets a person with a paid plan switch between Plus and Business, cancel, resume, and open the portal for the card and invoices. Sources: https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/upgrade-downgrade, https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/subscription-schedules, https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/subscription-schedules/use-cases ("Schedule an upgrade or downgrade for an existing subscription", "Release a subscription from a schedule"), https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/prorations, https://docs.stripe.com/api/subscriptions/update, https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/cancel, https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/webhooks.

- **Upgrade (Plus to Business), immediate:** `subscriptions.update` on the one subscription item (never a second item) with `proration_behavior: always_invoice`, which charges the difference now, and `payment_behavior: error_if_incomplete`, so a declined card returns an error and the plan is unchanged. The renewal date does not change. This does not support cards that need 3D Secure; those people see the "not changed" message and can use Manage billing. Unknown: whether `pending_if_incomplete` supports these updates with Stripe Tax, so it is not used.
- **Downgrade (Business to Plus), at the end of the period:** a subscription schedule. The app calls `subscriptionSchedules.create` with `from_subscription` (one phase that matches the current period), then `subscriptionSchedules.update` with that phase kept as is plus a second phase with the Plus price (`duration` one month, `end_behavior: release`, `proration_behavior: none` on the update and on the new phase). Nothing is charged, credited, or refunded, and the person keeps Business until the phase starts. Stripe's docs list Subscription Schedules as the way to change a subscription at the end of its billing period. A direct `subscriptions.update` with `create_prorations` would change the plan today and add a credit, which the owner no longer wants. The update repeats the phase's metadata and `automatic_tax` setting because Stripe unsets anything omitted. A subscription whose phase has a discount, extra invoice items, or a trial is refused with "Use Manage billing", because the app does not copy those. If any step fails, the schedule the app just made is released and the person is told nothing changed.
- **Where the pending change lives:** only in Stripe. The Billing page reads the subscription with `expand: ['schedule']` on each load and shows "Your plan changes to Plus on <date>". No table or migration holds a copy, so a reload, a webhook, or a second device always agrees with Stripe. If that read fails, the page shows no pending change and everything else still works.
- **Cancel the scheduled change (keep Business):** `subscriptionSchedules.release`, which Stripe documents as removing the schedule without canceling the subscription. Asking to switch to Business while a downgrade is pending does the same.
- **Cancel the subscription while a downgrade is pending:** the app releases the schedule first, then sets `cancel_at_period_end`, so the subscription ends at the period end on Business. What `cancel_at_period_end` does on a subscription that still has a schedule attached is Unknown in Stripe's docs, so the app never sets it while a schedule is attached. If the second step fails, the person is told the scheduled change was canceled and the cancellation did not go through.
- **Upgrade while a schedule is attached:** refused ("Cancel the scheduled change first"). Stripe says to change an attached subscription through the schedule API, and that direct changes may be overwritten at the next phase.
- **Not allowed in the app:** switching or downgrading while a payment is past due, while the subscription is set to end (resume first), when it is not active, for Enterprise licenses, and for organization licenses (organization billing stays in the portal). Cancelling a scheduled change is allowed in any state.
- **Cancel and resume:** `cancel_at_period_end` true or false. Resume works until the period ends; a subscription that has ended cannot be reactivated, so the person subscribes again.
- **Webhook:** the stored plan changes only when the subscription's price actually changes. Creating, editing, or releasing a schedule leaves the price alone, so those events change nothing. Stripe's pages say a new phase updates the subscription, but not whether that sends `customer.subscription.updated` (Unknown). So the endpoint handles both `customer.subscription.updated` (price in the items) and the new `subscription_schedule.updated` and `subscription_schedule.completed` events (the price of the current or last phase). Each event id is applied once, and an event older than the stored state is ignored. `subscription_schedule.created`, `.released`, `.canceled`, and `.aborted` are not needed: a canceled or aborted schedule ends the subscription, which arrives as `customer.subscription.deleted`.
- **Database:** no migration. The failed-payment grace period is unchanged at 7 days.

### Stripe setup checklist

Do these in test mode first, then live mode. Never commit keys, price ids, or promotion codes.

1. Products and prices (USD, monthly, tax behavior Exclusive): two products, "blueyvatar Plus" $9.00 and "blueyvatar Business" $49.00. Keep the old Plus and Pro prices active until their subscribers have moved.
2. Environment variables on the web app (Infisical `prod`, `STAGING_` prefix for staging, then `fly secrets set`): `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY` (a restricted key also needs Subscriptions write), `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`, `STRIPE_PRICE_PLUS` (new $9 price id first, then optionally the retired Plus and Pro price ids, comma separated), `STRIPE_PRICE_BUSINESS`. Delete `STRIPE_PRICE_PRO` if it is set. Optional: `STRIPE_ALLOW_PROMO_CODES=true` on staging only. `PUBLIC_URL=https://blueyvatar.com`.
3. Webhook endpoint `https://blueyvatar.com/api/stripe/webhook` (and the staging URL with test mode) subscribed to exactly: `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.created`, `customer.subscription.updated`, `customer.subscription.deleted`, `invoice.payment_failed`, `subscription_schedule.updated`, `subscription_schedule.completed`. Add the two `subscription_schedule` events to an existing endpoint before deploying the downgrade change.
4. Customer portal settings: payment method update on, invoice history on, cancel subscription on with "at end of billing period", return link `https://blueyvatar.com/account/billing`. Switch plan: off (see step 4 above).
5. Billing settings: Smart Retries on, customer emails for failed payments on, and when all retries fail, cancel the subscription, with the retry window inside 7 days.
6. Stripe Tax, invoice branding, and the support email, as in the Dashboard steps above.
7. Test in test mode: subscribe with Plus; switch to Business (card charged the difference); switch back (credit on the next invoice); cancel and confirm "Ends on" shows; resume; confirm card 4000 0000 0000 0341 on a switch shows the "not changed" message; confirm a failed renewal shows the grace notice.

### Testing with a free coupon

1. In Stripe test mode, open Product catalog, Coupons, and create a coupon with 100% off.
2. Choose a duration: "Once" covers the first invoice only, "Forever" covers every renewal.
3. Under redemption limits, set a maximum number of redemptions, for example 5.
4. Set a redeem-by date so the coupon expires on its own.
5. Open the coupon and create a promotion code for it. Optionally limit it to first-time customers.
6. Set `STRIPE_ALLOW_PROMO_CODES=true` on the web app. Checkout now shows the promotion code box.
7. Subscribe with the code, then unset the variable (or set it to `false`) when testing is done.

### Behavior to know

- **Webhook handling.** The endpoint verifies the signature against the raw request body (a 5 minute timestamp tolerance), then applies the event in one database transaction that also records the event id in `stripe_events`. A redelivery changes nothing. If an event cannot be applied (for example a subscription arrives before its customer is linked), the endpoint answers 500 and Stripe retries. Check Developers, Webhooks, Event deliveries for failures.
- **Tier mapping.** `active` and `trialing` subscriptions give the price's tier. A Plus price gives the Plus license tier and the Plus user plan; a Business price gives the Business license tier and also the Plus user plan for the person's own account. `past_due` keeps the tier during the grace period. `unpaid`, `canceled`, `incomplete`, `incomplete_expired`, and `paused` give Free. Enterprise licenses are contracts and billing never changes them. A price id that is not listed in `STRIPE_PRICE_PLUS` or `STRIPE_PRICE_BUSINESS` is ignored, unless the subscription is already stored, in which case the event still updates it under its stored plan so a cancellation is never lost. Rows and licenses that hold the retired plan name `pro` are read as Plus everywhere (web, delivery, indexer, webhook), and new Plus licenses are stored as `plus` in `licenses.tier` (migration 0036 renamed old `pro` licenses). The `pro` reads stay as a safety net for old rows and mixed deploys.
- **Grace period (proposal for the owner).** `PAST_DUE_GRACE_DAYS` in `packages/core/src/billing.ts` is 7. A license that goes past due keeps its paid tier for 7 days from the first failed payment, then serves at Free limits. Delivery and the dashboard evaluate the expiry on every read, so no webhook is needed. Data, avatars, and profiles are never deleted. The owner has not approved the number yet.
- **Plus.** Plus is $9 per month, approved by the owner, and combines the old Plus and Pro plans. A DID has Plus while it holds a Plus subscription, or a Business subscription on its own license, that is `active`, `trialing`, or `past_due` inside the same 7 day grace period, or while it is listed in `PLUS_DIDS`. `PLUS_DIDS` stays as a manual grant for testing and gifts and needs no subscription. The web app and delivery both read the subscription, so the gallery, extra avatar slots, and custom domain follow a purchase within a webhook delivery. A license holds one subscription at a time.
- **Overage.** Metered overage is not billed yet. It needs the per-miss cost measured after deploy, then a metered price and usage reporting.
